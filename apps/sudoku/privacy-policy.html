Privacy Policy

PixelBeast s.r.o. ("PixelBeast", "we", or "us") takes your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains how the Sudoku app collects and uses data.

1. What Data We Collect

We collect only application usage data through Firebase Analytics. We do not collect any personal user information.

Usage data includes:

  • Game events and interactions within the app
  • Session duration and frequency
  • Device type and operating system version
  • App crashes and performance data
  • Feature usage patterns

We do NOT collect:

  • Names or email addresses
  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Payment information
  • User profiles or accounts

2. How We Use This Data

We use application usage data solely to:

  • Improve the app: Understand which features are used and how to make the app better
  • Fix bugs: Identify and resolve crashes and performance issues
  • Monitor performance: Ensure the app runs smoothly for all users

3. Data Sharing

Your usage data is processed by Google Firebase, which is our analytics provider. Firebase data is subject to Google's privacy policies. We do not share or sell your data to any third parties.

4. Data Security

Firebase uses industry-standard security measures to protect data in transit and at rest. All data is encrypted during transmission via HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data Retention

Firebase Analytics retains data according to their standard retention policy. You can modify or delete your data through your device settings.

6. In-App Purchases & Payment Information

We do not handle or store payment information. All in-app purchases are processed securely by third-party payment platforms:

  • Apple App Store: Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. Your credit card and billing information are protected by Apple's secure payment system.
  • Google Play Store: Payment processing is handled entirely by Google. Your payment information is protected by Google's secure payment system.

The Sudoku app never has direct access to your credit card numbers, banking details, or sensitive payment information. All transactions are processed and secured by Apple or Google according to their privacy and security policies, which you can review on their respective websites.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is limited personal information to request or delete. However, you can:

  • Disable analytics: You can opt out of data collection through your device settings
  • Contact us: For any questions about your data

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:

9. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or by email. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Children's Privacy

The Sudoku app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We collect only anonymous usage data through Firebase Analytics.
