About the App

Sudoku is a classic logic puzzle that tests your reasoning and problem-solving skills. The app was developed by PixelBeast with a focus on clean and intuitive design.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player, Sudoku offers an interactive and engaging experience that will entertain you while exercising your mind.
